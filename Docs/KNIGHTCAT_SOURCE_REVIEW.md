# KnightCat reference review

Checked 19 October 2026. This records public evidence, not a legal determination of ownership or enforceability.

- The supplied cream/brown kitten in engraved silver armor, white cape, pink ear bow and oversized upright sword closely matches the character described as KnightCat/Kitty by the site presenting itself as the official home of sickmanai: https://knightcat.ai/lore
- That site credits Instagram @sickmanai as creator and identifies 20 August as the first Instagram post, followed by another viral post on 22 August. Some secondary sources report 19/21 August. The original Instagram reel URL and timestamp could not be independently verified through accessible public pages. The official site provides a copy labelled "The first video": https://knightcat.ai/videos
- The site asserts that its videos and artwork belong to the creator and requests permission before reposting: https://knightcat.ai/
- Its linked shop explicitly limits the free Kitty & Dragon wallpaper to personal use: https://sickmanai-shop.fourthwall.com/products/kitty-the-dragon-free-4k-wallpaper
- The shop's general terms provide a personal, noncommercial service license and reserve material rights. They are not a commercial character/animation license: https://sickmanai-shop.fourthwall.com/pages/terms-of-service
- Business and licensing inquiries are directed to the creator's Instagram account: https://knightcat.ai/contact and https://www.instagram.com/sickmanai/

No public grant covering use of the existing KnightCat character, AI-derived animation sprites, an ad-supported mobile game, or store/promotion artwork was found in the reviewed pages. No creator was contacted and no permission has been obtained. A creator's website claim alone is not independent proof of the full chain of rights, and AI-related protection can depend on jurisdiction and human authorship.

The original-reference sprite experiments remain outside the project and are not licensed or shipped game content. The user subsequently requested a new design retaining only the general fluffy kitten type: lighter cream fur, pink cape, a different armor model and a different sword. A text-only original-design prototype with sage-green eyes, pearl/rose-copper leaf armor, fern/crescent details, mauve tunic and a short sheathed ring-guard sword was generated outside the project. The original reference image was not used to generate this design's animation sheets. This is a design distinction, not a guarantee of legal clearance.

Final user constraint: use the character only if its use is permitted; otherwise do not use it. Since no applicable commercial grant was verified, all unfinished character integration code was removed. Neither the reference-based character nor the redesigned prototype is in the game, catalog, build, or GitHub. No new rescue milestone was added; the existing game remains unchanged. Research notes alone are retained.

If the existing KnightCat is desired instead, seek written permission from the relevant rights holder covering commercial Android distribution, AI-assisted adaptation/animation, promotional screenshots/video, permitted territories, duration, attribution and any fee. A wallpaper download or permission to repost a video does not establish those rights.
